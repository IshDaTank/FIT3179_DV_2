# Dataset scope and accuracy

- National Surfing Reserves (19 registered places; coordinates approximated to locality) — https://surfingreserves.org.au/national-surfing-reserves/
- World Surf League 2026 original announced calendar, July 2025 snapshot (12 venues; not actual or revised schedule) — https://www.abc.net.au/news/2025-07-29/world-surf-league-2026-schedule-announced/105584976 and https://surfingvic.com/rip-curl-pro-bells-beach-to-open-2026-wsl-championship-tour/
- Australian Sports Commission AusPlay 2025 surfing estimates — https://www.ausport.gov.au/clearinghouse/research/archive/surfing

**Dates changed after the initial tour announcement.** The map and global calendar intentionally visualise a documented announcement snapshot, not final completed results. The existing Australian stops dataset similarly uses announcement windows.

Participation is nationally estimated, not beach visitation; reserve status is recognition, not a beach quality index. No government buoy readings or popularity estimates are fabricated.
